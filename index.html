GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Are these physical books or digital downloads?

They are digital PDF workbooks. No physical item is shipped.

Can I type into the workbooks?

These PDFs include interactive fields and clickable navigation. Compatibility varies by PDF reader; a desktop PDF reader may offer the most reliable form-filling experience.

How will I receive my purchase?

Checkout is handled by Jim. Because Jim does not automatically notify this website of successful payments, the seller must verify and approve each purchase before delivering the PDF. Delivery is not instant or guaranteed at a particular time.

Can I buy more than one workbook?

Yes. Each workbook currently has its own $8.99 checkout link, so purchases are made separately.

Will these guides replace a wedding planner?

No. They are self-guided planning tools designed to help you organize information and make decisions; they do not replace professional venue, legal, financial or safety advice.

What if I have trouble with my purchase?

Keep your Jim payment confirmation and contact the seller through the contact method provided with your payment link. A dedicated support email should be added before public launch.
